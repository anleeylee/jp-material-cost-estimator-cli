日本語

材料価格スナップショットと都道府県別労務単価を組み合わせ、10% 消費税を分離計算して材料費・送料・労務費を出典ラベル付きで集計します。全国平均価格は主張しません。

  • Bundled snapshots and 20-occupation x 47-prefecture rates
  • 10% tax computed from both tax bases
  • Materials, shipping and labor kept separated
  • REGISTRY / USER_SUPPLIED provenance per line

English

Combines material price snapshots and prefectural labor rates, separates the 10% consumption tax, and totals materials, shipping and labor with provenance labels. No national average price is claimed.

  • Bundled snapshots and 20-occupation x 47-prefecture rates
  • 10% tax computed from both tax bases
  • Materials, shipping and labor kept separated
  • REGISTRY / USER_SUPPLIED provenance per line
